## 🎵 What Spotify Actually Offers

### Spotify's Meditation Features:
✅ **Partnership with Calm** - Free Sleep Stories, guided meditations  
✅ **Massive podcast library** - Thousands of free meditation podcasts  
✅ **Curated playlists** - "Calm for Sleep", "Calm for Stress & Anxiety"  
✅ **Music for focus/relaxation** - Ambient sounds, instrumental music  
✅ **Free tier available** - Much content accessible without premium  

### Spotify's LIMITATIONS:
❌ **Not meditation-focused** - Music platform first, wellness second  
❌ **No structured programs** - Just content, no guided journeys  
❌ **No progress tracking** - Can't track meditation habits or streaks  
❌ **No B2B/Corporate features** - No team management, analytics, or reporting  
❌ **No personalization for wellness** - Recommendations are music-focused  
❌ **Limited interactivity** - No timer, no customization, no breathing exercises  
❌ **No corporate analytics** - HR can't see employee engagement or ROI
