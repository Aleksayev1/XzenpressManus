## Tips for Apollo.io:

1. **Personalize** the {{firstName}}, {{company}}, and {{industry}} variables
2. **A/B test** different subject lines to see what performs best
3. **Schedule** emails during business hours (9 AM - 5 PM in their timezone)
4. **Use sequences** - Set up automatic follow-ups if they don't respond
5. **Track** open rates and adjust your approach accordingly
